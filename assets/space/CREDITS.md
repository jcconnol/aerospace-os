# Space imagery credits

These telescope photographs are bundled locally so backgrounds work offline. All stars, planets, rings, asteroids, portals, galaxy and black-hole visuals outside the two photographs are generated by the project.

## Lagoon Nebula

File: `lagoon-nebula.jpg` (unmodified screensize JPEG)

Credit: NASA, ESA, STScI

- Source: https://esahubble.org/images/heic1808a/

- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

In the app, Aerospace OS projects these images onto the sky, rotates them per folder, adjusts brightness, and feathers their edges into a procedural starfield. These transformations are artistic compositions; the scene does not represent true astronomical positions or scales. Credit is displayed on screen in the corresponding regions.
